# time (change 009)

Timesheets: hours logged by people and by agents against delivery projects, ISO weeks submitted for approval, approved through the manager or project chain, and locked once approved.

- **Owner:** `/modules/time/` in `.github/CODEOWNERS`
- **Screens:** `/time` and `/time/w/:week` (Team time), `/time/w/:week/p/:weekId` (one member's week, read-only, 073), `/time/my` and `/time/my/:week` (My timesheet). Weeks are ISO keys such as `2026-W42`.
- **Contracts** (`contracts.ts`): `weekTotals(tx, viewer, week)` and `projectHours(tx, viewer, range)`, which apply the caller's data scope (own time, plus weeks they could see on Team time; `projectHours` returns aggregates only), ISO week helpers (`isoWeekOf`, `weekStart`, `workDays`, `shiftWeek`, `weekKey`, `parseWeekKey`), `EVENTS`, `WEEK_STATUSES` and the event payload types. Version 1.
- **Events:** `time.entry.logged`, `time.entry.corrected`, `time.entry.deleted` (each carries the entry's `revision`, so a consumer keeps only the newest; 070), `time.week.submitted`, `time.week.approved`, `time.week.rejected`, `time.week.reopened`, `time.week.reminded`, `time.change.requested`, `time.change.decided`, `time.change.completed` (notification rules for submitted, approved, rejected and reminded weeks, and for entries someone else fixed, live in `module.ts`; 030 delivers them).
- **MCP tools** (`mcp.ts`): read `get_team_week`, `get_my_week`; write `log_time`, `correct_time_entry`, `delete_time_entry`, `submit_week`, `approve_week`, `reject_week`, `remind_week`, `reopen_week`, and for change requests `request_time_change`, `decide_change_request`, `answer_change_request`, `cancel_change_request` (read: `get_change_requests`). Time logged over MCP is agent time.
- **Chat intents** (`chat.ts`): log time, fix hours, remove hours. `get_my_week` and `get_timesheet` list the week's entries by id (an Admin sees them on others' weeks too), so chat names the exact entry.
- **Logging:** a day is required (YYYY-MM-DD in the person's calendar; the server doesn't guess "today" in UTC) and must be Monday to Friday; a day holds at most 24 h, in steps of 0.05 h. A submitted week can't change until the approver sends it back.
- **Corrections (070):** an entry's hours, billable flag, note or project can be fixed, or the entry deleted, in a draft or rejected week; the day never changes (delete and log again). The owner can do it, and so can a PM who approves the entry's project (and the new one, if it moves) or an Admin, who must give a `reason`; the owner is then notified (`time.entry-corrected-by-other`, `time.entry-deleted-by-other`). Corrections and deletes are refused on a submitted week and on an approved one, Admin included: reopen it with a reason first. A delete marks the row (`deleted_at`, `deleted_by`) and every read, total and guard skips it; the row and its audit stay. Chat's confirm card copies the entry's `project`, `day`, `hoursBefore` and `person`, and the tools' `propose` check refuses a card that doesn't match the stored entry. Undoing a commit-draft suggestion removes its entry, with the `time.entry.deleted` event.
- **Confirm cards (070 amendment 2):** the write tools give chat's card its words through `card` (`server/entry-card.ts`, `server/week-card.ts`): "Fix Fleet Tracker, Wed Oct 14" with before → after lines, "Remove …: 2.4 h", "Log 1.5 h to …", "Approve Nimal Silva's timesheet for 2026-W42 (31.5 h)", read from the stored records, never ids. Their `propose` checks refuse a card the service would refuse anyway: a fix or delete on a submitted or approved week, `log_time` on a submitted week or an approved one the caller can't change, approve or reject on a week that isn't submitted, remind on one that isn't draft or rejected, reopen on one that isn't approved or is your own. Chat then says why, and no card shows.
- **Change requests (070 amendment 3):** the owner of a submitted or approved week asks for one entry to be changed or removed (`request_time_change`, with a reason); nothing changes yet. Anyone in that week's approval chain (Admin, line manager, a PM with time on the week; never the owner) sees it on Team time and decides in chat: `apply` makes the change through the correction core, attributed to them with the request's reason and id, and the week keeps its status (on an approved week it's audited as `time.week.locked_change` with the request id); `reopen` sends a submitted week back or reopens an approved one (for the approval chain, through a request only; `reopen_week` stays Admin only), and the request completes when the week is submitted again; `ask` puts a question to the owner, who answers in chat; `decline` gives a reason. One open request per entry; `apply` is refused if the entry's revision moved since the request. Approvers hear of each request and answer; the owner hears of each question, decline, reopen or applied fix. Stored in `time.change_request` (`0004_change_request.sql`).
- **Chat on Time's pages (070 amendment 4):** `chatSuggestions` and `pageLabel` in `chat-suggestions.ts` give chat its header ("My timesheet · Week of Oct 12") and up to three suggestions per page, each shown only when the viewer holds its tool and its check passes on the week shown (e.g. "Fix an entry" on a draft week, "Ask for a change" on a locked one, "Remind everyone who hasn't submitted" for approvers). Rows carry pins and no per-row Fix buttons. `remind_weeks` and `approve_weeks` take the week ids from `get_team_week` and make one card; confirming is one service with a savepoint per week, so each person is done, skipped (with why) or failed on their own, and the card reports "Reminded 2 · skipped 0 · failed 0" with a line per person.
- **Weeks in writes:** submitting, approving, rejecting, reminding and reopening name the week (or a `weekId`); the server never picks one by its clock. A week you can't act on and a missing one give the same error, and nothing is locked before that check. Read tools still default to the latest week.
- **Legal-entity scope (071):** every check about another person's week passes the owner's `auth.user.legal_entity_id`: the approval chain (`chainFor`), `seesWeek`, `mayFix`, reopen, the locked-change rule, the contract reads, change requests, `remind_weeks`/`approve_weeks`, and the card and suggestion checks. For an entity-scoped viewer, an owner with no entity is out of scope (fails closed); unscoped grants are unchanged. Your own time is never entity-checked.
- **Payroll totals (071):** `time:totals` (granted to Payroll) and the contract read `approvedWeekHours(tx, viewer, { from, to, userIds? })`: approved weeks only, live entries only, per person and ISO week, with `unreadable` for people outside the reader's entity scope. No projects, days, notes or requests.
- **Utilisation scope (071):** `hoursVisibleTo(tx, viewer, { userIds, from, to })` is the contract Reports uses for whose hours a viewer sees: their own; `time:team` or an approver in the person's entity; a manager who approves time; a PM for people with hours on a project they manage.
- **One entry per source (071):** `time.entry.source_ref` (`git:<sha>` for a commit draft, `mcp:<clientRef>` for `log_time`'s `clientRef`), unique per person among live entries. A commit already logged isn't drafted again, and accepting a second suggestion for it logs nothing (`alreadyLogged`). A `log_time` retry with the same `clientRef` and input returns the first entry with `replayed: true` and writes nothing; other input is refused.
- **Possible duplicates (071):** a log for the same person, project, day and note as a live entry is flagged ("Already logged" on the card, `possibleDuplicateOf` in the result), never refused.
- **Entry rules (071):** `get_time_settings` / `set_time_settings` (Admin; `time.setting`). `requireAllocation`: log, accept a commit draft or move an entry only to a project the person is allocated to in that day's month (Allocations by hr person; can only be switched on with Allocations installed). `backdateWeeks` (1–52): the person's own time no more than N ISO weeks back; PM or Admin fixes with a reason and applied change requests are exempt. `allowFuture`: off refuses a day after the person's local today (entity zone). Defaults (off, none, on) reproduce Time before 071, and no chat card is made for a refused rule.
- **Weekly utilisation (072):** `get_week_utilisation` (week, optional `below`): per person the viewer may see, hours logged (a sent-back week counts none) ÷ their weekly target (`time.target`, 40 h unless an Admin sets one with `set_weekly_target`), overtime above 100% shown as is, the working days so far with nothing logged, and with Leave the leave-adjusted target and %; lowest first. `get_my_week` adds `target` and `utilisation`. Team time offers "Who is below 80% this week?" to approvers. This is the weekly figure; Reports' `get_utilisation` is monthly billable utilisation.
- **Approver routing (072):** `time.week.submitted` and `time.change.requested` carry `projectIds` and `legalEntityId`, so their notices reach the week's project approvers in the owner's entity.
- **Weekend hours (073):** `log_time` takes a Saturday or Sunday only with `weekend: true` (the card says "(weekend)"); weekend hours are ordinary hours of the ISO week, so they count in every total, and a week with them shows Sat and Sun columns marked "weekend". Weekend days are never "missing". A weekend commit's suggestion says so and logs with `weekend: true`.
- **Reminders (073):** `remind_missing` (approvers) reminds, in one card, everyone the viewer is responsible for who logged nothing on a working day: their reports, people with time or an allocation on a project they manage in the last 4 weeks, and for Admins and `time:team` people in their entities with time or an allocation in the last 4 weeks; anyone on approved leave or already logged is skipped. Optional message (default "Please update your hours for today."); the bell says "Log your hours for {Tue Oct 20}" and links to `/time/my/{week}?day=`, where that column is marked "Missing". `remind_week` takes the same message. Every reminder is recorded in `time.reminder`; Team time rows show the last one, and a card warns when someone was reminded in the last 4 hours. No reminders on Saturday or Sunday. Schedules (per legal entity, its own zone): `time.daily-reminders` every 15 minutes on weekdays sends once the Admin's `dailyReminderAt` (settings) has passed, one per person a day, skipping leave and people whose recent projects a PM switched off (`set_project_reminders`); `time.draft-reminders` on Monday 09:00 reminds last week's drafts and sent-back weeks, once per week.
- **Approval checks (073):** `submit_week`'s card and result list the working days with no hours and no leave ("Missing: Fri Oct 16"), and it still submits. A submitted week over 60 h, with a day over 14 h, or with missing days has `anomalies` on Team time, a "Check" line on its approve card, and is skipped by `approve_weeks` ("needs individual review"); when every week there is flagged, approve-all makes no card. ThisWeek's capacity is the allocated hours (Allocations' percent × each person's weekly target), or "allocation unknown"; its project rows pin to `get_team_week` with `project`.
- **Team grid (073 amendment 1):** Team time reads `teamGrid(viewer, week, query)` (`server/grid.ts`), one `teamWeek` read plus batched reads for entries, targets, leave, local today and HR departments. Each day cell shows its hours and a state with an icon and a word, scaled to the person's daily target (weekly target ÷ 5): Missing (a past working day with nothing), Low (under 75%), On target, Over (above 112.5%), Not yet, On leave, Weekend; focus, hover or tap lists that day's projects. A summary strip (team hours, average utilisation, people with missing days, weeks waiting for your approval), a Utilisation column, and a footer of daily totals. Filters (`?project=`, `?team=` from HR departments, `?status=` incl. `not_submitted`) and sort (`?sort=name|total|utilisation&dir=`) are URL-only; a project filter recalculates every figure from that project's hours. People the viewer expects to log (073's reminder rule) with no timesheet show as "Not started" with a Remind suggestion. A date beside the week arrows jumps to its week. A name opens `/time/w/:week/p/:weekId`; actions there are chat's. My timesheet marks days the same way: Missing (red) for past working days with nothing logged and no leave, and On leave (`server/my-days.ts`). Changes to Team time: rows sort by name by default, the table is restyled, and Not started rows appear. 100 people load in about 0.9 s on a dev machine (`tests/grid-perf.test.ts`, limit 2 s).
- **Approval rules:** you never approve your own week. Admin approves any week; a manager approves the weeks of their reports (`viewer.reports`, filled by hr when it's installed); a project manager approves weeks with time on a project they manage. Reject needs a comment. Approved weeks are locked: logging into one needs another Admin (never on your own week) and is audited as `time.week.locked_change`; correcting or deleting its entries needs it reopened first; and `reopen_week` takes a reason and, like approving, is never done on your own week.
- **Depends on:** delivery (projects, managed-project scope). **Integrates with:** allocations (071 entry rules), hr (reporting lines; without it only the project chain applies), leave (not wired yet; until it is, a week carries a free-text note such as "Casual leave Fri").
- **Allocated hours** on the screen are 40 h per person until allocations (010) publishes capacity.
- **Data:** `time.entry` (with `revision`), `time.week`, `time.approval` (RLS on `tenant_id`); retention entries in `module.ts`.
- **Tests:** `pnpm --filter @amax/time test` (DB tests need `DATABASE_MIGRATE_URL`); screen e2e: `pnpm --filter @amax/time e2e` after `pnpm --filter @amax/web build`.
