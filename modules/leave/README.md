# leave (change 012)

Leave requests, balances, the team calendar, holidays and approvals, with leave types and holidays from country packs.

- **Owner:** `/modules/leave/` in `.github/CODEOWNERS`
- **Screens:** `/leave` and `/leave/m/:month` (a calendar month such as `2026-10`). Stats, the team calendar with today and holidays, leave by type and per month, and the requests waiting for you with their release clash check. Every write is an Ask amax button; every stat, card and row has a pin.
- **Contracts** (`contracts.ts`), version 1: `approvedLeave(tx, reader, { from, to, personIds? })` for payroll (014) and time, `balancesFor(tx, reader, personId, year)`, `leaveVisibleTo(viewer, { userId, legalEntityId })` (072: Leave's own visibility rule, for Time's weekly utilisation and Reports to tell "leave not visible" from "no leave"), `LEAVE_TYPES`, `REQUEST_STATUSES`, `LEAVE_SENSITIVE`, `EVENTS` and the payload types. A reader is a viewer (own leave, reports' leave, and everyone's in the entities their `leave:team` reaches) or a system context core issued with `contextFor.job` (everything); a hand-built system context fails closed.
- **Events:** `leave.requested`, `leave.approved` (with `paid`, for payroll and time), `leave.rejected`, `leave.cancelled` (with `wasApproved`). Payroll (014) and time consume `leave.approved`; neither is wired yet.
- **MCP tools** (`mcp.ts`): read `get_leave_overview`, `get_team_calendar`, `get_calendar_row`, `get_leave_request`, `get_my_leave`, `list_holidays`; write `request_leave`, `approve_leave`, `reject_leave`, `cancel_leave`, `apply_leave_pack`.
- **Chat intents** (`chat.ts`): request leave.
- **Rules:** days are working days (Monday to Friday) less the holidays of the person's country; a half day (`am` or `pm`) is a single day of 0.5. A request needs the days left in its balance (entitlement plus carried, less taken before go-live, approved and pending days), except unpaid leave, and mustn't overlap the person's own pending or approved leave. The person's manager (the hr reporting line) or HR for the entity approves or rejects (reject needs a comment), never the person. The person cancels their own request until the leave has ended; HR cancels anyone's.
- **Country packs** (`packs.ts`): Sri Lanka (annual 14, casual 7, medical 7, lieu, no-pay; Poya and public holidays) and Australia (annual 20, personal/carer's 10, long service, unpaid; NSW public holidays). `apply_leave_pack` installs one for a legal entity. The holiday lists are the demo's 2026 Q4 dates: confirm each year's against the gazette (Sri Lanka) or the state's list (Australia). A full yearly calendar per country is a follow-up.
- **Medical details:** a request's medical reason and certificate are `health` (002): HR and the person see them, a manager sees the type only.
- **Clash checks** use delivery releases through delivery's contract: `delivery.release.deployed` keeps `leave.release_window`. A deployment short of Prod keeps the release in flight for 14 days; reaching Prod ends it. A pending request that overlaps a window clashes. **Integrates with delivery** (optional): without it no event arrives, nothing clashes and requests still work.
- **Depends on:** hr (people, legal entities, reporting lines).
- **"Today"** is the tenant's `leave.clock_pin` (the demo pins fixtures.md's Oct 16, 2026), else the real date.
- **Mock notes:** the mock's donut (716 days) contradicted its own per-month bars (620); the mock section now uses 620 for both. The unused-annual ticket shows days only: payroll (014) values the liability, since pay belongs to hr and payroll. Nimal's Oct 27–30 request overlaps two releases in flight in delivery's fixtures (Fleet Tracker v1.8.0 UAT retry and Contoso Portal v2.4.0 QA), so it shows both.
- **Data:** `leave.policy`, `leave.holiday`, `leave.balance`, `leave.request`, `leave.release_window`, `leave.clock_pin` (RLS on `tenant_id`); retention entries in `module.ts` (`leave.balance` holds only pseudonymous ids).
- **Tests:** `pnpm --filter @amax/leave test` (DB tests need `DATABASE_MIGRATE_URL`); screen e2e: `pnpm --filter @amax/leave e2e` after `pnpm --filter @amax/web build`.
