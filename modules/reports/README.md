# reports (change 027)

Utilisation and availability for delivery: billable hours ÷ available hours per person and for the team, the 12-month trend against a target, and who has time free for the rest of the month.

- **Owner:** `/modules/reports/` in `.github/CODEOWNERS`
- **Screen:** `/reports` ("Reports"), the mock's `001-amax-foundation-reports` section. The lede gives the team's utilisation for the month to date against the target; "Utilisation, last 12 months" draws the trend with the target line (a screen-reader table carries the values); the table card has two read-only tabs, Utilisation (available after leave and holidays, logged, billable, meter and %, and a Team row) and Availability (`/reports?view=availability`: working days left this month after holidays and approved leave, allocated %, free hours). Export downloads the open tab as CSV. "Change target" opens chat. Every card and row is pinned; there are no forms. The mock's third tab, Estimate vs actual, isn't built: no module's contract publishes estimates yet.
- **Utilisation (AC-1):** billable ÷ available. Available is, per working day (Monday to Friday, less the person's legal entity's holidays, within their hr employment), 8 h × FTE, less approved leave on those days (half days count half). Percentages are whole numbers.
- **Who sees what:** a report reads as the signed-in viewer and is never wider than the source modules. A person is counted only when (1) time's `hoursVisibleTo` lets the viewer see their hours (071: it's them; `time:team` or an approver in their legal entity; their manager with `time:approve`; or a PM for the weeks that hold hours on a project they approve, decided week by week with Time's seesWeek), and (2) hr's `listPeople` returns them to the viewer. Leave comes from leave's `approvedLeave` as the viewer; when leave is installed and the viewer may not see a person's leave (leave's own rule, read from Leave's contract `leaveVisibleTo`: the person, their manager, `leave:team` in their entity), their row shows "Leave not visible to you", no available hours, and stays out of the team figure. No leave reason is read; only the type ("2 days medical") of leave the viewer can already see.
- **Exports (AC-2):** CSV of exactly what the screen shows the viewer (the page's own read, or `get_utilisation` with `format: utilisation-csv | availability-csv`). Every cell is quoted, and one starting with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab or CR is prefixed with `'` so spreadsheets don't run it.
- **Hours read model:** time's contract gives per-person totals only per ISO week, so reports keeps `reports.logged_hours`, one row per time entry, filled from `time.entry.logged`, `time.entry.corrected` and `time.entry.deleted` (070). A row changes only for a newer entry `revision`, and a deleted entry stays as a tombstone (`deleted_at`) that reads skip, so late, repeated or out-of-order events never bring back old hours. Entries logged before reports was installed aren't copied. When time publishes a per-person range read, switch to it and drop the copy.
- **Which hours count (072, D1 option A):** utilisation counts hours whose timesheet week is submitted or approved. Each copied hour keeps its week (`logged_hours.week_id`), and `reports.week_status` follows Time's submitted, approved, rejected and reopened events, keeping only the newest by the event's time. Rows without a week (seeded, or copied before 072) count as before. A copied hour whose week this copy has no status for (submitted before 072 listened, or an event it missed) is looked up through Time's contract `weekStatuses(tx, viewer, weekIds)`, read as the viewer. If neither gives a status (Time no longer holds the week), the person's row is marked `hoursIncomplete` ("Some hours' timesheet status unknown"), shows no %, and stays out of the team figure. Optional modules (Time, Leave) count only when `hasModule` says this deployment runs them and their tables are there. The lede says so.
- **Holidays:** leave keeps its holiday calendar internal, so reports keeps `reports.holiday` per legal entity, seeded with the leave packs' Q4 2026 dates and changed with `set_holiday`.
- **Contracts** (`contracts.ts`): `utilisation(tx, viewer, { from, to })` → `{ rows: PersonUtilisation[], team: TeamUtilisation }` (for Insights 026 and the Overview), `EVENTS` and payload types.
- **Events:** emits `reports.target.set`, `reports.holiday.set`. Handles `time.entry.logged`, `time.entry.corrected`, `time.entry.deleted`, and `time.week.submitted`, `time.week.approved`, `time.week.rejected`, `time.week.reopened` (072).
- **MCP tools:** read `get_utilisation` (month, format), `get_person_utilisation` (userId, month); write `set_utilisation_target`, `set_holiday` (Admin, `reports:admin`).
- **Permissions:** `reports:read` for Leadership, PM and Finance; Admin holds everything. Finance has no `hr:read`, so it sees only itself until hr grants it.
- **Depends on** hr. **Integrates with** time (hours), leave (approved leave) and allocations (Availability), each optional: without leave, available hours take no leave off and the screen says so; without allocations, Allocated and Free show as not installed; without time, no new hours arrive.
- **Data:** `reports.logged_hours`, `reports.holiday`, `reports.settings` (target, default 80%, and the demo's pinned today), all with RLS on `tenant_id`. Retention: the hours copy is deleted seven years after the day, as timesheets are kept.
- **Fixtures:** fictional. Today is Oct 16, 2026. Nimal, Kasun and Ayesha's hours give fixtures.md's October table (96, 80 with 2 days medical, 88 with 1 day casual; 222 ÷ 264 = 84%) and the trend 72, 68, 74, 77, 79, 81, 78, 83, 82, 80, 84, 84.
