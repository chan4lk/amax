# finance (change 017)

Contracts per project, invoicing, receivables and overdue tracking. Agents draft invoices; Finance accepts them.

- **Owner:** `/modules/finance/` in `.github/CODEOWNERS`
- **Screen:** `/finance` ("Financials"). The lede says what needs attention (the next invoice a release unlocks, overdue invoices), then the Contract value, Invoiced to date and Overdue tiles, Invoiced per month against target (the current month hatched, "to date"), Receivables by age, and the Contracts table: kind and terms, value, invoiced meter, next invoice and payments. An open draft shows Accept draft and Query, and a ready retainer shows Invoice, each an "Ask amax" button that opens chat with the contract pinned. Filter (`?kind=`) and Download (CSV) are read-only. Every tile, card and row is pinned; there are no forms.
- **Contracts (AC-1):** `fixed` (milestones, optionally tied to a release version), `tm` (hourly rate; the value is the cap) and `retainer` (monthly fee; the value is the total). A contract is billed by a legal entity in its own currency. Each invoice stores its base-currency amount at the stored rate on its issue day (`finance.fx_rate`, `set_fx_rate`). Headline totals are in the tenant's reporting currency (`finance.settings.target_currency`), converted at stored rates (the inverse rate is used when only that one is stored); `byEntity` gives invoiced in each entity's base currency.
- **Billing agent (AC-2):** when time approves a week, the agent reads the month's approved billable hours per project through `@amax/time/contracts` as a read-only Finance reader, records them per T&M contract (`finance.billable`), and drafts the uninvoiced amount (hours × rate, within the cap). When delivery emits `delivery.release.deployed` to Prod, a fixed-price milestone tied to that version is drafted. Each draft becomes a `finance.invoice-draft` suggestion (006); accepting it issues the invoice; undo issues a full credit note while it's unpaid. A draft whose amount changes is superseded and suggested afresh.
- **Keeping up with time (072):** the month's T&M billable hours are recomputed from approved time on `time.week.approved` and `time.week.reopened`, and on `time.entry.logged`/`corrected`/`deleted` with `lockedChange: true` (an Admin logging into an approved week, or a change request applied to one). One recompute upserts per contract and period, so a retried or repeated event changes nothing; an invoiced period keeps its invoice and the difference is an open draft.
- **Tax (AC-3):** `server/tax.ts`, per the billing entity's country: Australia GST 10%; Sri Lanka SSCL 2.5% then VAT 18% on the SSCL-inclusive value; `export` contracts are zero-rated. Credit notes (`issue_credit_note`) carry negative amounts and tax in proportion, and reduce invoiced and open amounts. Rates are the demo's; check them with your adviser before use.
- **Contracts** (`contracts.ts`): `CONTRACT_KINDS`, `KIND_LABELS`, `listContractRefs(tx, reader)` (value, invoiced and open per contract, for readers with `finance:read` in the entity or system code), `EVENTS` and payload types.
- **Events:** `finance.contract.created`, `finance.invoice.drafted` (handled by its own draft agent), `finance.invoice.issued`, `finance.credit-note.issued`, `finance.payment.recorded`. Handles `time.week.approved`, `time.week.reopened`, `time.entry.logged`, `time.entry.corrected` and `time.entry.deleted` (072: locked changes and reopened weeks), and `delivery.release.deployed`.
- **MCP tools:** read `get_financials`, `list_contracts`, `get_contract`, `get_invoice`; write `create_contract`, `add_contract_milestone`, `set_fx_rate`, `invoice_retainer`, `record_payment`, `issue_credit_note`. Drafts are accepted or queried with 006's `accept_agent_suggestion` and `query_agent_suggestion`.
- **Permissions:** Finance: `finance:read`, `finance:contract:write`, `finance:invoice:draft`, `finance:invoice:issue`, `finance:payment:record`. Leadership: `finance:read`. Admin: all. Anyone else: 404.
- **Depends on** delivery (007) and agents (006). **Integrates with** time (009), optionally: without it, T&M drafts come only from hours already recorded.
- **Data:** `finance.settings`, `fx_rate`, `contract`, `milestone`, `invoice` (credit notes included), `payment`, `billable`, `draft`, all with RLS on `tenant_id` and amounts `numeric(19,4)`. Retention: who created, issued and recorded, seven years.
- **Fixtures:** fixtures.md's three contracts, billed by Lumen Labs Lanka to overseas clients (zero-rated exports): Contoso Portal fixed $420,000 (55% invoiced, the next $60,000 when v2.4.0 reaches Prod), Fleet Tracker T&M at $45/h capped at $210,000 (72% invoiced; INV-0912 $18,315 for September hours, due Oct 7, 9 days overdue; $14,200 of October hours drafted), HR Insights retainer $12,000 a month from Jul 1 (Jul to Sep invoiced and paid). Totals: $774,000 contract value, $418,200 invoiced (54%), $18,315 overdue, invoiced per month 61, 74, 58, 82, 77 and 26 ($k), target 80. The demo clock is pinned to Oct 16, 2026. Exchange rates are fictional demo rates. All fictional.
