# payroll (change 014)

The country-agnostic pay engine: runs with steps, the payroll agent's explanations of every change since last period, payslips and the bank file. Country rules plug in as packs (015 Sri Lanka, 016 Australia).

- **Owner:** `/modules/payroll/` in `.github/CODEOWNERS`
- **Screen:** `/payroll`. The run's step bar leads, then its totals (gross, net to banks, statutory), the changes since last period with the agent's explanation and Accept or Query ("Ask amax"), where the money goes, the payroll cost line, the payslips (with "Approve run" disabled while a change is open or queried) and totals by cost centre. A payslip opens in a drawer from its link (`?slip=`); `?run=` picks a run. Every step, tile, row and card is pinned; there are no forms.
- **Steps (AC-1):** Draft → Agent check → HR approval → Finance approval → Bank file → Payslips sent. Each is an audited `defineService` and an MCP write tool: `draft_payroll_run`, `check_payroll_run`, `approve_payroll_run_hr`, `approve_payroll_run_finance`, `send_payslips`. The bank file is the 006 kind `payroll.bank-file`: irreversible, two different people with `payroll:bank-file:approve` approve it and the second makes it. Only its row count, total and digest are kept; account numbers stay in hr.
- **Agent (AC-2):** checking a run records each person whose net pay moved since the last paid run, with a rule-based explanation from the payslip lines that changed (no model call). After the check, the payroll agent makes one `payroll.variance` suggestion per change. Accepting it marks the change accepted; querying or dismissing leaves it open. HR can't approve while any change is undecided. A change whose amount is unchanged on a redraft keeps its decision.
- **Visibility (AC-3, 002):** `server/read.ts` `levelOf`. Payroll and Admin (`pay` class) see every amount; Finance (`payroll:totals`) sees totals by entity and cost centre, with cost centres under 3 people folded together; HR sees the steps and the explanations with amounts masked; anyone else gets 404 on the screen and refusals from the tools. Anyone reads their own payslip once it's sent (`my_payslips`, `get_payslip`). Read tools declare their pay fields `sensitive`, so chat redacts them per the tenant's AI policy.
- **Country packs (AC-4):** `packs.ts`: `{ id, label, countries, fictional?, calculate(input), statutory(run), filings(run) }`, registered with `registerPack` from `@amax/payroll/contracts`. The engine gathers the inputs: base salary and cost centre from hr, approved unpaid leave from leave, overtime from approved timesheets when time is installed (time's `approvedWeekHours`, 071: totals only; people outside the drafter's entity are flagged `overtime-unknown` and the run's Sources line says the time read was incomplete), and the pay group's own components. The **demo pack** (`fixtures/demo-pack.ts`) uses made-up rates and labels say so ("Demo pension", "Demo income tax"); it runs the engine until 015 and 016 land.
- **Contracts** (`contracts.ts`): `STEPS`, `STEP_LABELS`, `MIN_COST_CENTRE`, `listRunRefs(reader)` (steps and size, never an amount), `runTotals(reader, runId)` (totals by cost centre, needs `payroll:totals` or pay), the pack API, `EVENTS` and payload types.
- **Events:** `payroll.run.drafted`, `payroll.run.checked` (+ `changes`), `payroll.run.hr-approved`, `payroll.run.finance-approved`, `payroll.run.bank-file`, `payroll.payslips.sent` (+ `payslips`, `userIds`). No event carries an amount. Handles its own `checked` (variance agent) and `finance-approved` (bank-file agent).
- **MCP tools:** read `list_payroll_runs`, `get_payroll_run`, `get_pay_change`, `get_payslip`, `my_payslips`; write as above. Accept and query go through 006's `accept_agent_suggestion` and `query_agent_suggestion`.
- **Permissions:** Payroll: read, draft, check, decide changes, approve the bank file, send payslips. HR: read, approve for HR, decide changes. Finance: read, totals, approve for Finance. Everyone: `payroll:payslip:read` (own payslips only).
- **Depends on** hr (011), leave (012) and agents (006). **Integrates with** time (009), optionally: without it the draft says overtime didn't come from timesheets.
- **Data:** `payroll.pay_group`, `pay_group_member`, `pay_component`, `run`, `payslip`, `payslip_line`, `change`, `bank_file` (all RLS on `tenant_id`, amounts `numeric(19,4)`). Retention in `module.ts`: pay records seven years from the period's end, each with its legal entity column so a country pack's statutory minimum (032) can lengthen it.
- **Fixtures:** one demo pay group for Lumen Labs Lanka on the demo pack (8 named people), September paid and October checked, with the changes from fixtures.md (Nimal's overtime, Dinesh's first salary, Ayesha's no-pay days to check, Rizwan's settlement). Amounts follow the demo pack, not the Sri Lanka figures in fixtures.md (those arrive with 015). All fictional.
- **Tests:** `pnpm --filter @amax/payroll test` (DB tests need `DATABASE_MIGRATE_URL`); screen: `pnpm --filter @amax/web build && pnpm --filter @amax/payroll e2e`.
