# notifications (change 030)

Turns other modules' domain events into notifications: an in-app item behind the bell, plus email and Microsoft Teams messages, following each person's preferences, digest and quiet hours. Modules never send messages themselves.

- **Owner:** `/modules/notifications/` in `.github/CODEOWNERS`
- **Screens:** the bell in the top bar (`topBar` in the manifest): unread count, the latest 20 unread first and grouped by module, each linking to its record; new items are announced through the live region and the list refreshes every minute while the page is open. `/notifications` (in the profile menu, not the rail): channels per category, digest and quiet hours, read-only, changed through chat.
- **Rules** (`NotificationRule` in `@amax/core`): a module lists `notifications` in its manifest: the event, an optional `when` match on the payload, the category (`approvals`, `releases`, `reminders`), urgency, recipients (`{ role }`, `{ owner: <payload field> }`, `{ approvers: <payload field> }` = that person's managers, else the Admins), an optional `permission` and `scope` the recipient must hold for the record, `except` (who acted is not told), and `title`, `body` and `href` templates. `{field}` fills a payload value and `{field|name}` turns a user id into a name. Time (submitted, approved, rejected, reminded), leave (requested to the approver chain; approved and declined to the person) and delivery (release rolled back, urgent, to the project's PMs) declare rules today.
- **Approvers (072):** `{ approvers: "userId" }` is the subject's managers who could approve, else the Admins. Optional `projects: "<field>"` adds everyone holding the rule's permission on one of the payload's projects (not those who approve every project, such as Admins, who stay the fallback), and `entity: "<field>"` keeps every approver within the subject's legal entity (no value fails closed). Time's submission and change-request rules use both.
- **Masking (AC-4):** a template field shows only if its emitter declared it `plain` in `events.payloads`, or the recipient may see its 002 class (`canSee`: pay is Payroll, Admin and the person themself). Anything else, including undeclared fields, renders as `•••`, in the bell and in every email and Teams message.
- **How it runs:** `events.handlesFrom` builds one outbox handler per event any installed module has a rule for (003 outbox, at least once). The handler resolves recipients as viewers (their roles and data scopes) and calls the `notify` service, which writes one notification per event per person (`unique (tenant_id, event_id, user_id)`) and one delivery per chosen channel. Each pending delivery is a 029 run (`notifications.deliver`): it sleeps through the person's quiet hours, sends with retries (5 attempts, backoff from 30 s), and records the outcome through `recordDelivery` (audited: who, which channel, which adapter, sent, failed or skipped). Digest deliveries wait for `notifications.digest`, which runs every half hour in UTC and sends to the people for whom it is now 08:00 to 08:29 in their legal entity's zone (so half-hour offsets such as Colombo's work, and an entity without a zone falls back to UTC instead of holding a schedule). Urgent items skip the digest but still wait for quiet hours.
- **Channels** (`server/adapters.ts`, one `ChannelAdapter` interface): email through Microsoft Graph `sendMail` (secret `msgraph-mail`, JSON `{ tenant, clientId, clientSecret, sender }`), Teams through an incoming webhook (secret `teams`, the webhook URL; the message names the person). Both go out through `@amax/api/egress` (public https only, pinned to the checked address) with secrets from 031. A channel with no credential is recorded as skipped, never retried. Slack, SMTP or a Teams bot is another adapter.
- **Defaults:** no preference row means email on, Teams off, no digest, no quiet hours. The bell always shows new items. Quiet hours are read in the person's legal entity's zone (`entityZone` from `@amax/workflows`).
- **Contracts** (`contracts.ts`): `CATEGORIES`, `CHANNELS`, `EVENTS`, the inbox and preference types, `unreadCount(tx, viewer)` (the viewer's own). Version 1.
- **Events:** `notifications.notification.created`, `notifications.delivery.recorded`, `notifications.preference.set`, `notifications.quiet_hours.set`, `notifications.notification.read`.
- **MCP tools** (`mcp.ts`): read `get_notifications`, `get_notification` (every bell item's pin), `get_notification_preferences` (the page's rows and cards); write `set_notification_preference`, `set_quiet_hours`, `mark_notifications_read`, each behind chat's confirm. Everything is the signed-in person's own.
- **Data:** `notifications.notification`, `notifications.delivery`, `notifications.preference`, `notifications.quiet_hours` (RLS on `tenant_id`); retention entries in `module.ts`. Email addresses are looked up at send time, never stored.
- **Tests:** `pnpm --filter @amax/notifications test` (DB tests need `DATABASE_MIGRATE_URL`); the bell and the page in the signed-in e2e (`apps/web/tests/e2e/notifications.spec.ts`).
